## 🌟 1. Executive System Overview

**EduWorld** is a unified, next-generation intelligent educational operating system spanning primary K-12 education, secondary national examination prep (JAMB UTME / WAEC WASSCE), university and postdoctoral quantum computing research, and full institutional governance across 8 independent role tiers.

### Architectural Highlights
- **8 Dedicated Persona Tiers**: Distinct design tokens, typography, and tailored workflows per user archetype.
- **Product Wedge Engine**: High-velocity computer-based testing (CBT) diagnostic simulator for JAMB & WAEC candidates with Item Response Theory (IRT) scoring.
- **Global Command Palette (`⌘K`)**: Instant search and routing across all 32 pages and role capabilities.
- **Interactive Demo Switcher**: Seamless 1-click simulation with factory-reset state management and live persona switching.
- **Multi-Currency System**: Automatic dynamic currency switching across USD (`$`), Nigerian Naira (`₦`), and British Pounds (`£`).
- **WCAG 2.1 AAA Accessibility**: High-contrast color tokens, full keyboard navigability, semantic HTML5, and screen-reader optimizations.

## 🧭 2. Global Navigation & Quick Access Systems

### 2.1 The Global Command Palette (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`)
Press `⌘K` (macOS) or `Ctrl+K` (Windows/Linux) anywhere in the application to open the unified search modal:
- **Instant Filtering**: Search by screen title, role tier (e.g., `Achiever`, `Scholar`, `Parent`), or capability (e.g., `diagnostic`, `rubric`, `intake`, `billing`).
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Use `↑` / `↓` arrow keys to highlight results, and `↵ Enter` to launch immediately.
- **Escape**: Press `ESC` or click outside to dismiss.

### 2.2 Floating Interactive Demo Switcher (Bottom-Right)
Located in the bottom right corner of every page:
1. **Pill View**: Shows active persona avatar, name, tier badge, and primary metric.
2. **Minimize/Expand**: Click the minimize `—` button to collapse into a compact floating badge.
3. **Switch Role Modal**: Click **"Switch Role"** to open an 8-card grid showing real-time live profiles with biographical context and benchmark stats.
4. **Reset Demo Data**: Click **"Reset Data"** to clear local storage overrides and restore default testing state.

### 2.3 Notifications Dispatcher & Popover
Click the notification bell icon in any dashboard header:
- Real-time unread counter with animated indicator.
- Categorized alerts: `Academic`, `Reward`, `System`, `Billing`.
- Click any notification to navigate directly to the relevant workspace.
- Click **"Mark all read"** to clear the unread badge.
